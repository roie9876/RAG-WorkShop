
# Station 35 (Kiryat Arie) Technical Specification

## Overview

Station 35 is an underground metro station serving the Kiryat Arie business district 
in Petah Tikva. It is the northernmost station on the initial M1 segment.

## Station Specifications

- **Depth**: 22 meters below ground level
- **Platform Length**: 150 meters
- **Platform Width**: 10 meters
- **Daily Passenger Capacity**: 35,000 passengers
- **Peak Hour Capacity**: 6,500 passengers
- **Entrances**: 2 (Business District and Residential)

## Connected Systems

Station 35 depends on the following infrastructure:
- **Ventilation System A**: SHARED with Station 36 (critical dependency!)
- **Central Power Grid**: Connected via Transformer 35A
- **Fire Suppression System**: Connected to Station 36's system
- **Signaling System**: Controlled by Central Traffic Control

## Adjacent Stations

- **South**: Station 36 (Shderot HaTzionut) - 1.2 km tunnel distance

## Shared Infrastructure Warning

⚠️ **CRITICAL**: Station 35 shares Ventilation System A with Station 36.
If Ventilation System A fails:
- Both Station 35 and Station 36 lose ventilation
- Both stations may require evacuation
- Passengers from both stations affected: ~80,000 daily

## Construction Details

- **Contractor**: Shapir Engineering (same contractor as Station 36)
- **Construction Period**: 2019-2024
- **Method**: Cut-and-cover 
- **Project Manager**: Yossi Cohen (Shapir Engineering)
