
# Incident Report: Ventilation Failure - March 15, 2026

## Incident Summary

On March 15, 2026 at 08:47 local time, Ventilation System A experienced a complete 
failure affecting both Station 35 and Station 36. Both stations were evacuated as 
a precaution, disrupting morning rush hour service for approximately 2 hours.

## Timeline

- 08:47: Ventilation System A main fans shut down unexpectedly
- 08:48: SCADA alerts triggered at NTA Control Center
- 08:49: Station 35 air quality sensors detect reduced airflow
- 08:50: Station 36 air quality sensors detect reduced airflow
- 08:52: Emergency generators activate automatically
- 08:55: Decision made to evacuate BOTH stations
- 09:00: Station 35 evacuation begins (3,200 passengers)
- 09:05: Station 36 evacuation begins (4,100 passengers)
- 09:25: Both stations confirmed empty
- 09:30: Climatec Systems technicians arrive on site
- 10:15: Root cause identified - power supply unit failure
- 10:45: Temporary repair completed, system restarted
- 11:00: Station 35 reopened
- 11:05: Station 36 reopened

## Root Cause

The main power supply unit for Ventilation System A failed due to capacitor 
degradation. The unit was 18 months past its recommended replacement date.

## Impact

- **Duration**: 2 hours 13 minutes of service disruption
- **Passengers evacuated**: 7,300 (3,200 from Station 35 + 4,100 from Station 36)
- **Trains affected**: 12 trains rerouted or held
- **Estimated economic impact**: ₪450,000

## Key Finding: Shared System Cascade

This incident demonstrates the cascade effect of shared infrastructure:
1. Single failure point (Ventilation System A power supply)
2. Affected TWO stations (Station 35 AND Station 36)
3. Combined evacuation of 7,300+ passengers
4. Lesson: Shared systems require redundant power supplies

## Action Items

1. [Climatec Systems] Replace all power supply units on ventilation systems
2. [NTA] Add redundant power supply to Ventilation System A
3. [NTA] Review all shared systems for single points of failure
4. [Shapir Engineering] Update emergency procedures for multi-station events
5. [Training] Conduct joint evacuation drill for Stations 35 and 36
