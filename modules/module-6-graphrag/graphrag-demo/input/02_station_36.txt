
# Station 36 (Shderot HaTzionut) Technical Specification

## Overview

Station 36 (שדרות הציונות) is a deep underground metro station located at the 
intersection of Jabotinsky Street and Shderot HaTzionut in Petah Tikva. It serves 
the Nachalat Yehuda neighborhood and surrounding residential areas.

## Station Specifications

- **Depth**: 25 meters below ground level
- **Platform Length**: 150 meters
- **Platform Width**: 12 meters  
- **Daily Passenger Capacity**: 45,000 passengers
- **Peak Hour Capacity**: 8,500 passengers
- **Entrances**: 3 (North, South, and Central Plaza)

## Connected Systems

Station 36 depends on the following infrastructure:
- **Ventilation System A**: Shared with Station 35 (critical system)
- **Central Power Grid**: Connected via Transformer 36A
- **Fire Suppression System**: Connected to Station 35's system
- **Signaling System**: Controlled by Central Traffic Control

## Adjacent Stations

- **North**: Station 35 (Kiryat Arie) - 1.2 km tunnel distance
- **South**: Station 37 (Central Hub) - 0.8 km tunnel distance

## Failure Modes

If Station 36 becomes unavailable:
1. Passengers must use Station 35 or Station 37 as alternatives
2. If Ventilation System A fails, Station 35 is also affected (shared system)
3. Train service may continue through the station without stopping
4. Emergency evacuation routes lead to both Station 35 and surface exits

## Construction Details

- **Contractor**: Shapir Engineering
- **Construction Period**: 2019-2024
- **Method**: Cut-and-cover with TBM tunnel connections
- **Project Manager**: Yossi Cohen (Shapir Engineering)
