
# Israel M1 Metro Line - System Overview

The M1 Metro Line is Israel's first underground metro system, serving the Tel Aviv 
metropolitan area. The line runs from Petah Tikva in the east to Tel Aviv's central 
business district, with planned extensions to Bat Yam in the south.

## Stations on the Northern Segment

The northern segment includes the following stations:

1. **Station 35 (Kiryat Arie)**: Underground station serving the Kiryat Arie business 
   district. Connects to Station 36 to the south. Shares Ventilation System A with 
   Station 36 due to their proximity.

2. **Station 36 (Shderot HaTzionut / שדרות הציונות)**: Deep underground station at 
   the intersection of Jabotinsky and HaTzionut. This station connects to Station 35 
   to the north and Station 37 to the south.

3. **Station 37 (Central Hub)**: Major interchange station connecting M1 to future 
   M2 and M3 lines. All northern stations feed passenger traffic into this hub.

## System Dependencies

Critical infrastructure relationships:
- Stations 35 and 36 share Ventilation System A (if it fails, both stations affected)
- All stations connect to Central Power Grid via the main transformer
- The Signaling System controls train movements between all stations
- Emergency systems are interconnected for coordinated evacuations

## Construction Contractors

- Shapir Engineering: Responsible for Station 35, Station 36, and tunneling
- Solel Boneh: Responsible for Station 37 (Central Hub)
- NTA Metropolitan Mass Transit: Overall project management
